## 2. Information We Collect

### Account Information

When you sign in with Google, PromptCanvas may receive basic account information provided by Google, including:

- Name
- Email address
- Profile picture
- Google account identifier

We use this information to authenticate you, create or manage your PromptCanvas account, and provide account-related features.

PromptCanvas does not receive or store your Google account password.

### App Preferences and Activity

PromptCanvas may store information required to provide app features, including:

- Favorite or saved prompts
- Notification preferences
- Account preferences
- Other actions required to provide features you choose to use

We do not collect browsing or activity information beyond what is necessary to operate, maintain, secure, and improve PromptCanvas.

### Device and Technical Information

PromptCanvas may process limited technical information necessary for app operation, including:

- App-generated device identifier
- Device platform
- App version
- Push notification token
- Notification settings
- IP address and basic request information processed by our servers or service providers

We do not intentionally collect precise GPS location data.

Some service providers may infer an approximate location from information such as an IP address as part of providing, securing, or operating their services.

### Push Notifications

If notifications are enabled, PromptCanvas may send notifications about:

- New prompts
- Featured content
- App-related updates
- Other relevant PromptCanvas information

PromptCanvas uses push notification services including Expo and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).

These services may process push notification tokens and limited device information required to deliver notifications.

You can disable Prompt Drops within PromptCanvas or disable notifications through your device settings.

## 3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

- Authenticate users
- Create and manage PromptCanvas accounts
- Provide PromptCanvas features
- Save favorites and preferences
- Send push notifications when enabled
- Maintain security and prevent misuse
- Diagnose technical issues
- Maintain and improve app reliability and performance
- Respond to support and privacy requests

We do not sell personal information.

## 4. Third-Party Services

PromptCanvas relies on third-party services that may process information as necessary to provide their services.

### Google Sign-In

Google Sign-In is used to authenticate users.

Google may process authentication and account information according to Google's own privacy policies and terms.

### Expo

PromptCanvas uses Expo services for parts of the mobile application infrastructure and push notification delivery.

Expo may process push notification tokens and technical information required to provide these services.

### Firebase Cloud Messaging

Firebase Cloud Messaging is used to deliver push notifications to Android devices.

FCM may process device and push notification identifiers required to deliver notifications.

### MongoDB Atlas

PromptCanvas uses MongoDB Atlas as cloud database infrastructure for storing application information such as account-related data, preferences, favorites, sessions, and push notification registration information.

These service providers process information according to their own privacy policies and contractual terms.
