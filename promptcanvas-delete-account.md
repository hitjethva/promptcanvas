# Delete Your PromptCanvas Account

PromptCanvas users can request deletion of their account and associated data.

## How to Request Account Deletion

Send an email to:

hitjethva@gmail.com

Use the subject:

PromptCanvas Account Deletion Request

Please send the request from the same email address associated with your PromptCanvas account.

## Data That Will Be Deleted

When your account deletion request is completed, we may delete:

- PromptCanvas account information
- Name
- Email address
- Profile information
- Saved favorites
- Notification preferences
- Device and push notification registration information
- Other account-related application data

## Data That May Be Retained

Some limited information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or regulatory purposes.

## Processing Time

Account deletion requests will be reviewed and processed within a reasonable period after identity verification.
